I had the privilege of working at ONTOP Studios, an AR indie studio, as part of a small, fast-moving team of passionate developers. I worked across the full development cycle, from scrappy early prototypes to fully shipped titles.

Working primarily in Unity, my responsibilities spanned the full stack; from backend to UI and gameplay programming while collaborating closely with design and art to keep production running smoothly.

We shipped to iOS and Android, prototyped for Oculus Quest and Apple Vision Pro, and worked with a proprietary web-based AR engine. These are some of the projects I worked on.
